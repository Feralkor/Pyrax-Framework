# Pyrax Framework — Solution Archetypes

Status: CANONICAL
Version: 0.3

## Purpose

Provide reusable problem-shape patterns without assuming a specific industry.

## Archetypes

### Exception Management
Use when the main problem is identifying what needs attention now.
Typical patterns: aging, threshold breach, bottleneck detection, severity classification.

### Predictive Operations
Use when the objective is anticipating a near-future operational condition.
Typical patterns: rate of change, dead reckoning, scenario projection, capacity risk.

### Constraint & Coverage
Use when demand, capacity, stock, time or resources compete.
Typical patterns: coverage, deficit propagation, constraint propagation, allocation guardrails.

### Asset & Condition Intelligence
Use for machines, vehicles, infrastructure or other assets.
Typical patterns: state reconstruction, degradation signals, baseline deviation, intervention timing.

### Customer / Account Intelligence
Use when decisions concern customers, contracts, accounts or portfolios.
Typical patterns: behavior change, concentration, renewal risk, margin pressure, service degradation.

### Workflow & Process Intelligence
Use when queues, handoffs, cycle time or rework are the main concern.
Typical patterns: aging, queue pressure, lead-time decomposition, bottleneck detection, rework signals.

### Commercial & Revenue Intelligence
Use for pipeline, pricing, conversion, revenue, margin and retention decisions.
Typical patterns: funnel aging, rate of change, baseline deviation, concentration, scenario projection.

### Risk & Compliance Intelligence
Use when the main objective is detecting exposure before violation or loss.
Typical patterns: threshold/rule breach, missing evidence, deadline aging, constraint propagation, confidence gating.

### Capacity & Resource Intelligence
Use when people, equipment, compute, space or other resources must be balanced against demand.
Typical patterns: utilization, coverage, queue pressure, capacity risk, scenario projection.

### Strategic Intelligence
Use when decisions aggregate multiple operational/business signals into scenario-based management support.
Typical patterns: trend, baseline deviation, concentration, scenario projection, evidence synthesis.

## Selection rule

A product may combine archetypes, but the first vertical slice should identify one primary archetype and one decision owner.

Archetypes are starting hypotheses, not certified semantics.

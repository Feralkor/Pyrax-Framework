# Hypothetical Pattern — Strategicos Predictive Intelligence

> Example only. This is not a claim about Strategicos Group systems, data or internal processes.

## Possible objective
Anticipate commercial, operational or financial conditions that deserve management attention before they become obvious in lagging indicators.

## Candidate entities
- Client
- Contract
- Opportunity
- Project
- Revenue
- Cost
- Ticket/Issue
- Business Unit
- Tax/Regulatory Exposure

## Candidate source categories
- CRM
- ERP
- Finance
- Project management
- Service/support
- External regulatory or market data

## Example signal chain
`Observed Client/Business State -> Trend/Constraint Signals -> Projected Risk or Opportunity -> Candidate Management Action -> Evidence -> Assisted Strategic Decision`

## Candidate deterministic-first methods
- aging;
- rate of change;
- rolling windows;
- threshold breaches;
- concentration analysis;
- baseline deviation;
- constraint propagation;
- scenario projection.

## Example decision-support record
Signal: margin deterioration over a sustained comparable window.

Evidence: revenue trend, cost trend, service burden, contract timing and data coverage.

Candidate action: review commercial/service strategy before renewal or planning cycle.

Confidence: component-level and evidence-backed.

## Framework lesson
The architecture does not change. The fine-tuning is the ontology, sources, semantics, signals, decision policies and UX.
